L59: 
L60: Largest reference drug list ranked by the risk for developing DILI in humans
L61: The DILIrank 2.0 dataset comprises 1,336 FDA-approved drugs, expanding from 1,036 drugs in DILIrank 1.0 with the addition of 300 drugs approved between 2010 and 2021. Each drug is assigned to one of four categories based on its potential to cause drug-induced liver injury (DILI). The classification is derived from a comprehensive review of hepatotoxicity descriptions in FDA-approved labeling and causality evidence from the literature.
L62: As the largest publicly available annotated DILI dataset, DILIrank 2.0 categorizes drugs into three groups with established causal relationships but varying levels of DILI severity (vMost-DILI-concern, vLess-DILI-concern, and vNo-DILI-concern) and one group with undetermined causality (Ambiguous-DILI-concern). Among the original drugs, 49 were reclassified in DILIrank 2.0, resulting in the following distribution:
L63:   * 217 vMost-DILI-concern drugs
L64:   * 351 vLess-DILI-concern drugs
L65:   * 414 vNo-DILI-concern drugs
L66:   * 354 Ambiguous-DILI-concern drugs
